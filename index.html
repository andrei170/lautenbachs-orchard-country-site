Our Story

Seventy years on the same Highway 42 address.

Bill and Ruth Lautenbach chose a 100-acre stretch of the Door County peninsula in 1955 and planted it with cherry trees. What began as a family orchard has grown into five interconnected operations, each one rooted in the same ground, the same family, and the same stretch of Highway 42.

Today Erin Lautenbach leads as General Manager and Owner, with Carrie Lautenbach-Viste heading Sales and Marketing alongside siblings Chris and Melissa. The third generation carries forward what Bill and Ruth built, and what Bob, the second generation, stewarded after them.
